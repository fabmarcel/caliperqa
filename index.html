Who you are hiring

I am Fabián Arce. Nineteen years in enterprise systems, eleven of them in quality engineering. Before that I built ETL pipelines and reporting systems for Procter & Gamble, which is why reconciliation work comes naturally: I have been on both sides of the data, building the pipeline and then breaking it.

Most of my career has been spent on software that moves money. Four years owning quality end to end on a digital banking platform: accounts, authorization, interbank transfers, check processing and the reconciliation between them. Then Salesforce CPQ and Logik.io at Teradyne, from product configuration and pricing rules to approvals, bundles, discounts and everything downstream to invoicing.

Most of what I find lives at the edges. The order with one line item priced at zero. The renewal that crosses a fiscal year. The customer record with an apostrophe in the name. Happy paths get tested by everyone; the cases that reach production are the ones nobody thought to write down.

I work from Heredia, Costa Rica, in US time zones, and contract as an independent professional.
